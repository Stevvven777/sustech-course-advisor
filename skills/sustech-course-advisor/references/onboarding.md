# First-use interview

Use short batches and show what was inferred before asking.

1. Read `tis degree progress --details`, then show the inferred cohort, major, plan code, and current term. Ask the student to confirm any track or exceptional program that TIS does not establish.
2. Resolve and read the official PDF. Present the extracted current-semester frame: required courses, choice pools, recommended sequencing, and manual-review rules. Do not continue until the student confirms the document identity; confirmation of the identity does not resolve ambiguous clauses.
3. Ask for minimum, target, and maximum credits; must-take and excluded courses; blocked periods; early/late, laboratory, and campus tolerance.
4. Ask for interest phrases rather than forcing a taxonomy. Accept course codes as strong interest signals.
5. Ask whether the student has direct experience with a complete teaching team or wants to avoid one. Store this separately from NCES evidence.
6. Summarize the framework and allow corrections before writing the profile.

Offer reasonable defaults, but never silently choose a curriculum version, track, maximum credit load, or interpretation of a conditional requirement.
